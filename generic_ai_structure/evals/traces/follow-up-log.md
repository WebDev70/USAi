# Follow-Up Log

Use this log for deferred work, advisory gaps, and process improvements that are not completed in the current task.

## Policy

- Blocking gaps should not be deferred unless an accountable owner and explicit acceptance decision exist.
- Advisory gaps may be deferred only when they have an owner, due date or review cadence, and clear success criteria.
- Repeated gaps should be converted into prompt, eval, checklist, dataset, tool, doc, or backlog updates.
